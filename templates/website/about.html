Our Mandate

The Vision

To raise a supernatural company of believers rooted in the infallible word of God, walking in apostolic authority, expressing divine love, and demonstrating kingdom dominion in leadership, family, marketplace, and ministry.

Our Assignment

The Mission

Preaching the uncompromised gospel of Jesus Christ, building spiritual capacity through systematic biblical teaching, cultivating an atmosphere of deep Holy Spirit communion, and equipping saints for global evangelism.

Foundations of Faith

What We Believe

1. The Infallibility of the Holy Scriptures

We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, authoritative, and infallible Word of God, serving as the supreme standard of faith and righteous living.

2. The Eternal Godhead & Trinity

We believe in one eternal God manifested in three co-equal persons: the Father, the Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit.

3. Salvation by Grace through Faith

We believe salvation is obtained solely through faith in the atoning sacrifice, bodily resurrection, and lordship of Jesus Christ.

4. The Power & Gifts of the Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, the active operation of spiritual gifts, divine healing, and apostolic signs and wonders.
