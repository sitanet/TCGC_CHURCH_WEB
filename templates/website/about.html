Apostolic Foundations

The Doctrine of Christ & What We Believe

1. The Incorruptible Seed of the Holy Scriptures

We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, authoritative, and living Word of God. Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the Word of God which liveth and abideth forever (1 Peter 1:23).

2. The Revelation of the Mystery of Christ & The Godhead

We believe in the one eternal God revealed in the Father, the Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit. We contend for the unveiling of the Mystery of Christ—which in other ages was not made known unto the sons of men, that the Gentiles should be fellowheirs and partakers of His promise in Christ (Ephesians 3:4-6).

3. Redemption, Grace & Awakening to Righteousness

We believe redemption is the sovereign love and justice of God accomplished in Christ. Through grace by faith, the believer is justified, awakened to righteousness, and born again in the spirit and soul into eternal life.

4. Ascension into the Service of the Most Holy

We believe believers are called to transit from carnal living into spiritual maturity—entering into the Holy of Holies by the blood of Jesus, yielding to the inward operation of the Spirit, and serving the living God in truth and holiness.

5. Strong Discipleship Culture & Christ-Centered Families

We believe the local church must be built through intentional apostolic discipleship. We champion godly marriages, authentic manhood and womanhood in God's kingdom, and raising generations that carry an incorruptible heritage.
