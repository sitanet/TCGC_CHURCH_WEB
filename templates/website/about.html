Our Story & Heritage

Rooted in Faith, Raising Kingdom Champions

The CityGate Church is an apostolic and prophetic ministry commissioned to equip believers, reveal Christ in all dimensions, and manifest Kingdom light to our generation.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of spiritually mature, biblically sound, and culturally relevant believers who dominate in their spheres of influence through kingdom principles.

Our Mission

To preach the uncompromised gospel of Jesus Christ, establish believers in kingdom authority, build strong families, and impact communities through acts of grace and love.

What We Believe

  • The Scriptures: The Bible is the inspired and infallible Word of God, our supreme rule of faith and practice.
  • The Godhead: We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • Salvation: Salvation is by grace through faith in the sacrificial death and resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  • The Holy Spirit: We believe in the baptism and present-day ministry of the Holy Spirit with spiritual gifts and empowerment.
