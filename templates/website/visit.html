Visitor Experience

What to Expect at The CityGate

What is the service experience like?

Our gatherings are dynamic, spirit-filled, and word-centered. You will experience vibrant praise and worship, uncompromised biblical exposition, prophetic insight, and warm fellowship.

What should I wear?

Come as you are! You will see people in traditional attire, formal suits, business casual, and relaxed clothing. We care about your presence, not your wardrobe.

Is there Children’s Church available?

Yes! Our dedicated CityGate Kids Church offers safe, engaging, and age-appropriate Bible classes, praise, and activities for infants through teenagers.

Where do I park?

Our traffic stewards and protocol team will guide you into the secured church parking grounds upon arrival at our main gates.
