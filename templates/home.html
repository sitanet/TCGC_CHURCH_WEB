WELCOME NOTE

We’re glad to have you join us here! We are a people committed to pressing into God’s promise for man and attaining the peak of Hi desire - men embodying the life of His Only Begotten Son, Jesus Christ and growing up into His fullness, which is achieved through the unveiling of the mysteries of Christ, the Father and God.

Our mandate is to equip the saints and make them fully rooted in the will of God so they will not be tossed around by every wind of doctrine but be persuaded in their quest to arrive in God.

We welcome you to join us in this lifetime experience of learning God and His ways. Kindly maximize the media resources in our archives and subscribe to our media channels for updates on upcoming events.
Stay Blessed.
